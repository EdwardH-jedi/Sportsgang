HOW IT WORKS
From a good fit to a plan you both agreed.
Five clear steps. A chat needs you both, and a session needs a yes.
1
Set your preferences
Choose running, golf or both. Runners
add a pace range or choose social
running, plus distances and times.
Golfers add their handicap situation,
experience, holes and who they want to
play with.
2
Show interest
Browse people whose preferences fit
yours, with the reasons on every card.
Interest is private.
3
Chat when itʼs mutual
A chat opens only when you both show
interest. That is where you work out the
details.
4
Propose a session
Suggest a time and place from the chat.
They confirm or decline, and until then it
stays pending.
5
See it in My Plans
Confirmed sessions, pending proposals
and the group runs and rounds you host
or join, in Sydney time.

Prefer a group? Join a run or round in Explore while spots are left, or host one. There is
no group chat: the host sets the time, place and number of spots, and joining adds it to
My Plans.

FAQ
Straight answers.
Where can I get SportsGang?
SportsGang is an iPhone app on theApp Store
(https://apps.apple.com/au/app/sportsgang/id6767027447). It is built for Sydney first, and
times in the app are Sydney time.
Is the running and golf experience already in the app?
It arrives with the next SportsGang update. The version on the App Store today is an earlier
release that works differently, so features on this page such as pace ranges, golf partner
preferences and My Plans come with that update.
Do I have to share my pace or my handicap?
No. Runners can choose social running instead of a pace range, and golfers can say they
have no handicap. Cards say so plainly when something hasnʼt been shared, instead of
guessing.
Are handicaps verified?
No. Handicaps are self-reported and not verified, and an estimate is labelled as an estimate.
Will beginners be matched with experienced golfers?
Only with golfers who have said they are happy to play with beginners or are open to any
level. Experienced golfers who havenʼt said so are not shown to someone who wants to
learn.

Can anyone message me?
No. A one-to-one chat opens only when you both show interest. Group runs and rounds
donʼt have a group chat; joining one adds it to My Plans.
Does SportsGang book tee times, track runs or offer coaching?
No. SportsGang doesnʼt book courses or tee times, doesnʼt track runs or record routes, and
doesnʼt provide coaching. It helps you find people and agree a time and place.
What if someone makes me uncomfortable?
Report or block them from a chat or their profile. Once blocked, neither of you can message,
propose a session or show interest, and you wonʼt see each other in Explore.
Is this a dating app?
No. SportsGang is for finding people to run or play golf with, around a session you both
agree on.
